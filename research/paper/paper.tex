\begin{abstract}
Modern enterprise compliance auditing requires analyzing complex legal contracts, security policies, and service level agreements across heterogeneous document formats. Standard keyword search suffers from vocabulary mismatch, whereas pure dense vector retrieval struggles with exact numerical and entity matching. We present an Enterprise-Grade Multi-Document Intelligence \& Compliance Auditor, integrating BM25 lexical search, FAISS dense vector retrieval, Cross-Encoder reranking, grounded Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) with abstention handling, deterministic compliance evaluation, and cross-document contradiction detection. Evaluated across a 102-query benchmark dataset, our hybrid retrieval pipeline achieves 20.1\% Recall@5 and 0.231 nDCG@5, maintaining sub-12ms hybrid search latencies on local CPU infrastructure.
\end{abstract}

\begin{equation}
S_{\text{Hybrid}}(d) = w_{\text{bm25}} \cdot \hat{S}_{\text{BM25}}(d) + w_{\text{dense}} \cdot \hat{S}_{\text{Dense}}(d)
