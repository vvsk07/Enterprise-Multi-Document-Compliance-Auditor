CORPORATE INFORMATION SECURITY POLICY - POLICY A

Document ID: POL-2026-A
Effective Date: March 1, 2026
Organization: Global Enterprise Systems Inc.

1. Termination Notice Requirements
Under Policy A, all enterprise service providers and contractors must provide at least thirty (30) days advance written notice prior to terminating any active operational contract.

2. Document and Audit Retention
All customer data logs, financial transactions, and compliance audit records must be retained for a mandatory period of two (2) years from creation date.

3. Contract Expiration Date
The master vendor framework agreement expires on December 31, 2026.
