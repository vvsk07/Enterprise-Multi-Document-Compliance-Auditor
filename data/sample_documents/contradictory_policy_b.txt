VENDOR COMPLIANCE SCHEDULE - POLICY B

Document ID: POL-2026-B
Effective Date: March 1, 2026
Organization: Global Enterprise Systems Inc.

1. Termination Notice Requirements
Under Policy B vendor requirements, all enterprise service providers and contractors must provide at least sixty (60) days advance written notice prior to terminating any active operational contract.

2. Document and Audit Retention
All customer data logs, financial transactions, and compliance audit records must be retained for a mandatory period of five (5) years from creation date.

3. Contract Expiration Date
The master vendor framework agreement expires on December 31, 2027.
